Final Chapter • Read this slowly. 💜

A really, really long birthday letter for Oxy.

Oxy, happy birthday. 💜

I could have written you a tiny message. “Happy birthday, Oxy. Hope you have a great day.” Done. Easy. Normal. Simple. But you and I have never exactly been a simple story, have we? So I wanted to make something that actually feels like you opened a little world made only for this day.

Somewhere inside all these buttons, hearts, gifts, stars, ridiculous little animations and dramatic words, there is one very real thing: I genuinely care about you. And because it is your birthday, I wanted you to feel that care instead of just seeing a sentence on your screen.

When I think about how we started, it still feels unreal. You were from Pakistan. I was from India. We met in Blockman Go — of all places — and somehow a random game became the doorway into a story that would end up carrying so many feelings. We started talking there. We got comfortable. We joked. We got annoyed. We argued. We made up. We fell apart. We came back. We tried again. At different times we probably wondered whether it would actually last, whether we were too different, whether the distance was too much, whether our personalities were too much, or whether we were simply two stubborn people who had no idea what they were doing.

But even when everything went wrong, you were never just “some random person” to me. You became a name I recognized instantly. A notification that could change my mood. A person I could be angry at and still miss. A person I could argue with and still secretly hope would message again. That says something.

And yes, we had our fights. A lot of them. 😭 Some were silly, some were serious, some were probably caused by two people who were overthinking everything, and some were the kind where both of us were basically like, “I am absolutely NOT apologizing first.” Looking back, I don't want to glorify those fights. I want to remember what they taught us. That feelings can become messy when people don't communicate. That caring about someone doesn't automatically mean you know how to love them perfectly. That sometimes people need patience more than they need another argument. And that saying sorry, listening properly, and choosing kindness can matter more than proving who was right.

Then came the breakups. More than once, we tried to end something that somehow kept finding its way back to life. We said goodbye and then, somehow, there was another conversation. There was another attempt. Another “maybe.” Another patch-up. Another reason to keep the door open. After what feels like millions of attempts, we're together again in some form, and honestly, I don't take that for granted.

Before Instagram, before our conversations moved somewhere new, before the story expanded, we were already carrying something from that original little game. And when we finally switched to Instagram, it felt like taking something that started inside a game and letting it exist in the real world. We were no longer just two avatars talking. We were two actual people with actual feelings, actual lives, actual moods, actual bad days, and actual reasons to care whether the other person was okay.

I think that's one of the things I want to tell you most on your birthday: I hope you are okay. Not “okay” in the fake way people say it when they don't want to explain anything. I mean genuinely okay. I hope your mind gets quieter. I hope the things that stress you out become easier to handle. I hope you have someone to talk to when you need it. I hope you don't keep every difficult feeling locked inside yourself. I hope you remember that needing support does not make you weak.

I hope you take care of yourself too. Eat when you're hungry. Sleep when you're tired. Rest before your body forces you to rest. Laugh when something is funny. Put your phone down sometimes. Go outside. Give yourself a break from trying to be everything for everyone. You don't need to earn the right to feel okay. You deserve ordinary happiness too.

I also hope you are proud of yourself this year. Not only for huge achievements. Be proud of the small ones. The days you kept going. The moments you handled something better than you would have before. The times you chose not to give up. The moments when you were kind even though you were tired. The things nobody else saw. Your progress counts even when it is quiet.

And since this is supposed to be a little romantic and a little playful too… I hope you know there is still a part of me that smiles when I see your name. There is still a part of me that remembers the version of us that could be ridiculously happy over the smallest thing. There is still a part of me that thinks it is kind of cute that all this started in a game. There is still a part of me that wants to tease you when you're being annoying. And, unfortunately for you, there is definitely a part of me that still wants to care for you when you are having a bad day.

We were lovers once. Then we weren't. We were strangers for moments. Then we found each other again. We have changed. You have changed. I have changed. Maybe what comes next won't look exactly like the past. Maybe that's okay. Maybe the best part of a second chance isn't recreating the old relationship. Maybe it's creating something healthier with everything we learned from the first version.

I don't need to pretend that I know exactly where our story is going. I don't. But I know I am grateful that, after everything, I can be here wishing you happy birthday. I am grateful that there is still a “we” somewhere in this story. I am grateful that I can still make something silly and emotional and over-the-top for you and genuinely mean every word underneath the drama.

So, Oxy, on your birthday, I wish you a year filled with things that feel real. Real happiness. Real laughter. Real friendships. Real growth. Real opportunities. Real peace. And, selfishly, a few beautiful moments that somehow include me too. 🥺

I wish you mornings that don't feel heavy. Nights that don't feel lonely. Conversations that make you smile. Music that hits at exactly the right time. People who understand you without making you beg to be understood. I wish you the courage to walk away from things that hurt you and the wisdom to recognize the things worth holding onto.

I wish you success, but I also wish you balance. I wish you ambition, but also rest. I wish you confidence, but also softness. I wish you strength, but also people with whom you never have to pretend to be strong. I wish you every kind of happiness that doesn't need to be photographed to be real.

And I hope you never forget that your existence has touched someone else's life. Mine. For better, for worse, through every messy chapter, you mattered. You still matter. That's why this page is so long. That's why I kept adding another button, another message, another memory, another stupid little heart. Because “happy birthday” didn't feel big enough for everything I wanted to say.

Thank you for the memories. Thank you for the laughter. Thank you for every conversation that lasted longer than expected. Thank you even for some of the arguments, because they became lessons. Thank you for coming back. Thank you for letting me come back. Thank you for the weird little history that only makes sense when someone says, “Wait… how did you two even meet?” And we get to say, “Blockman Go.” 😭🎮

If this birthday brings you only one thing from me, let it be this: you are cared for. You don't have to perform for it. You don't have to be perfect for it. You don't have to win every day for it. You can simply be Oxy — and that is enough for me to wish that your life becomes kinder, brighter and happier.

So happy birthday, Oxy. Please smile today. Laugh a lot. Let yourself be spoiled a little. Eat something nice. Enjoy your day. And somewhere between all the birthday wishes you get from everyone else, I hope you remember this one from Huzy — the girl from India who met you in a game, argued with you, loved you, lost you, found you again, and still wanted to build you an entire birthday universe because apparently one little message was never going to be enough. 💜

Whatever the future decides to do with our story, I will always be grateful that once upon a time, two people found each other in the most unexpected place. And today, on your birthday, I am simply happy that I get to say: I'm glad you exist, Oxy.

Happy Birthday, my Oxy. 🎂💜
